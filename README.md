##  Project Overview
[This project analyzes the Global Terrorism Database (GTD) to identify patterns and trends in terrorist incidents worldwide.
It explores changes over time, geographical distribution, attack types, and targeted sectors.
Python will be used for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and visualization.
The goal is to extract meaningful insights into terrorism trends and their human impact.]

---

## Project Objectives
- [Analyze Global Terrorism Trends: Identify trends and patterns in terrorist attacks across different countries and years (2000–2017).]  
- [Identify High-Risk Regions: Determine the countries and regions most affected by terrorist activities.]  
- [Evaluate Attack Characteristics and Impact: Analyze attack methods, target types, weapons used, and their impact in terms of casualties and damage.]  
- [Generate Data-Driven Insights: Use Python, SQL, and Power BI to extract meaningful insights and present them through interactive dashboards to support better understanding of global terrorism patterns.]
